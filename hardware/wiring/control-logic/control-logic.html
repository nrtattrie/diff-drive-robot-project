Due, motor-driver, and encoder control logic

Diagram

Complete installed control and feedback wiring among the Arduino Due, Pololu #2507, both #4753 encoders, and 74LVC245 translator.
This diagram covers P8, P9, and S2 through S7.
Use the owned Mega Prototype Shield V3 as the translator carrier, one #2204 socket, one #735, and owned Elegoo/Dupont leads.
Logic jumper colors and installed lengths are not yet assigned, so non-factory conductors render neutrally.
Encoder outputs are 5 V on the translator A side and must never connect directly to the 3.3 V-only Due.
Driver B motor terminals are not logic ground; motor power wiring is documented separately.
