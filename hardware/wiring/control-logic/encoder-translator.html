Encoder translator and Arduino Due wiring

Diagram

Focused P8, P9, S6, and S7 wiring for both #4753 encoders, the 74LVC245, and Arduino Due.
This focused view is intentionally redundant with control-logic.yml and motor-wire-groups.yml.
Use the owned Mega Prototype Shield V3 as carrier, one #2204 socket, one #735 translator,
two marked 1x6 header segments for the factory encoder housings, and owned low-current jumper wire.
The complete 74LVC245 DIP pinout is shown, including grounded unused A inputs,
open unused B outputs, fixed DIR and /OE straps, and local decoupling.
