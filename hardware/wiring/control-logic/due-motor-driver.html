Arduino Due and motor-driver logic

Diagram

Focused P9 and S2-S5 wiring between the Arduino Due and Pololu #2507 logic header.
This focused view is intentionally redundant with control-logic.yml.
Use twelve owned Elegoo male-to-female Dupont leads: male at the Due sockets and female at the #2507 header.
Jumper colors and installed lengths remain unassigned.
Motor supply and motor-output conductors are documented in the power and motor-encoder folders.
